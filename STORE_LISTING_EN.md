**Full description (max 4000, 3415 now):**
```
A photo or video from your phone carries more than you see: the exact place it was taken, the phone model, the camera serial number, the author's name and the time. MetaCheck shows what your file reveals and removes it before you send it.

WHEN IT HELPS
• Selling something online: remove your home location from item photos and videos before posting.
• Dating apps and social networks: make sure your photos don't reveal where you live or work.
• Sending files to strangers, landlords or clients: share a clean copy, keep the original.
• Journalists, activists and anyone who values privacy: see exactly what a file reveals before it leaves your phone.
• Photographers: view camera settings, lens and EXIF data, or edit the author and date in a copy.

PHOTO PRIVACY
• Privacy score 0–100 and a list of what the recipient will see: coordinates, serial number, author, capture time.
• "Send without metadata" in the Share menu: pick it in your gallery or any app and the file goes out already cleaned.
• Metadata cleaning: a copy without EXIF, GPS and XMP. The original file is not changed. GPS is removed from JPEG without re-compression.
• Shows where the photo was taken and, when you tap the button, the point on a map.

VIDEO PRIVACY (MP4, MOV)
• See the filming location, phone model, recording date and hidden tracks such as timecode.
• Pro cleans videos without re-encoding: picture and sound are copied byte for byte, so the quality stays exactly the same, even for long 4K videos.
• Works with videos from Android phones and iPhone.

ALL METADATA
• EXIF: camera, lens, shutter speed, aperture, ISO, dates and more, with field search.
• XMP, IPTC, PNG text chunks and video container tags. Export the report as PDF, JSON or CSV.
• Edit EXIF (date, camera, author, coordinates) in a JPEG copy.

AI LABELS IN THE FILE
• Content Credentials (C2PA): "created by AI" and "edited with AI" labels from ChatGPT, Gemini, Adobe Firefly, Sora and others, in photos and videos.
• IPTC labels, Stable Diffusion, ComfyUI and NovelAI generation parameters, and about 70 generator names.
• Only labels inside the file are checked; the picture itself is not analyzed. Social networks and messengers strip labels, so for such files the app honestly says there is no data. Missing labels do not prove a file is real.

FREE AND PRO
Free: analysis of photos and videos, metadata viewer, privacy score, GPS Privacy Shield, cleaning and editing up to 10 photos per day, a history of up to 10 reports.
Pro (one-time purchase, no subscription): no daily limit, cleaning videos (MP4, MOV) without re-encoding, batch cleaning of up to 50 photos, PDF and JSON export, a history of up to 50 reports, sending a report to your own API.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Analysis and cleaning run on your device. Photos and videos are never uploaded.
• The map (MapTiler, OpenStreetMap data) loads only when you tap its button. Once on, the map provider sees your IP address and where the point is on the map.
• No accounts, no ads, no analytics.
• Sending a JSON report to your own server is off by default and works only on your command.

HOW TO USE
1. Tap "Check a photo or video" and pick a file, or use the camera or clipboard.
2. See the privacy score and what the file reveals.
3. Tap "Clean and share" or "Remove GPS", and send the clean copy.

Choose the original file: screenshots and files sent through messengers often lose their metadata.
```
